# Glide Autoscroll privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Glide Autoscroll ("Glide") is a browser extension that adds middle-click autoscrolling to web
pages. This policy explains what it does with information.

**In short: Glide doesn't collect, sell or share any personal data. It has no servers and makes
no network requests.**

## What Glide uses, and why

- **Mouse and keyboard input on the pages you visit.** When you press the middle mouse button,
  Glide uses the pointer position and button presses to scroll the page, and a key press, click
  or scroll-wheel turn to stop. This happens inside your browser, in memory, only while you are
  scrolling. It is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
- **Page layout.** To decide what to scroll, Glide checks which parts of the page can scroll at
  the moment you middle-click. It does not read, keep or send the text or other content of pages.

## What Glide stores

- **Your settings**: speed, acceleration, dead zone, click style, display options, and the list
  of sites where you have switched Glide off. These are saved in your browser's extension
  storage. If you have browser sync turned on, your browser syncs them to your other devices
  through your browser account, under your browser provider's privacy policy. The developer
  never receives them.
- **A random identifier** in local extension storage that lets Glide's own scripts in different
  frames of a page recognise each other. It contains no information about you.

You can change or clear your settings on Glide's settings page at any time. Removing the
extension deletes everything it stored.

## What Glide doesn't do

- No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party code
- No network requests of any kind
- No selling or sharing of data, and no use of data for anything other than autoscrolling, in
  line with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements

## Permissions

- **Read and change your data on all websites**: needed so autoscroll works on any page you
  visit, including embedded frames. Glide uses it only to scroll.
- **Storage**: to save your settings.
- **Scripting**: so Glide starts working in tabs that were already open when it was installed,
  updated or switched back on, without you reloading them.
